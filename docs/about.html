About and contact

I am a researcher, engineer and entrepreneur based in Brisbane, Australia. My work connects behavioural economics, complexity science, artificial intelligence and engineering to understand how people, organisations and technologies interact—and to translate those insights into useful research, software and decisions.

I currently work as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Queensland University of Technology, in the ARC Training Centre for Behavioural Insights for Technology Adoption. I also contribute to interdisciplinary applied AI and technology-adoption research through QUT collaborations. Separately, I am Co-Founder and CEO of Panalogy Lab, an independent applied AI research and development company.

My academic and engineering background has shaped a practical, interdisciplinary approach to research. I am interested in connecting theory with robust empirical analysis, thoughtfully designed computational methods and real-world testing.

I supervise and mentor higher degree research students, research assistants and student project teams.

My experience includes Python, R, Stata, MATLAB, SQL/PostgreSQL, Django REST Framework, React, Streamlit, API integration, AWS-hosted research applications and Git/GitHub. My research methods include applied statistics and econometrics, behavioural experiments, agent-based modelling, network analysis, scientometrics, survey research, machine learning and human-centred AI evaluation.
