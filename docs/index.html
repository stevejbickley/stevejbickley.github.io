Hi, I’m Steve.

I study how people and technology shape one another.

I’m Dr Steve J. Bickley, a Brisbane-based researcher and engineer working across applied AI, behavioural economics and complex systems.

At QUT and ARC BITA, I explore how people make decisions, adopt technology and adapt to change. As co-founder of Panalogy Lab, I also help turn research into useful software and industrial applications.
