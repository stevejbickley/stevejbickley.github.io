The website uses metadata-free WebP derivatives. The original portrait assets have 640 px and 1280 px responsive versions. New event photographs live in `assets/images/events/`, with thumbnails limited to the original width and a larger compressed derivative at the source dimensions. No source photograph is enlarged during conversion. Original JPEG files and their metadata are not published.

Event photographs preserve the full image rather than cropping people or laboratory displays. Each has descriptive alternative text, explicit dimensions, lazy loading and a caption. Click or activate a photograph to open the larger image in the same keyboard-accessible viewer used for thesis figures. The image link also works without JavaScript.
